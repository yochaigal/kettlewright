### What Everyone Knows

Decades ago, Marius Lockwood, heir to a wealthy family, returned to his familial holdings with his wife Luciana and young daughter Anna. Less than a year later, the estate fell silent, and little was heard from the family again.

Characters should be able to recognize well-known family crests such as that of the Lockwoods. Meanwhile, rumors abound regarding a new waterborne illness that slowly kills its victims by filling their lungs with moss.

![Family Crest](https://cairnrpg.com/adventures/first-party/img/cas-4/family-crest.webp)

### White Ravens

Popular among those who live close to the Wood, the followers of the White Raven (or “the Flock,” as they prefer to refer to themselves) believe strongly in the power of the Wood and in keeping its forests and creatures healthy. They can be identified by tattoos of a white bird on their wrists, necks, or backs. Every settlement keeps a Flock unique to their community, and their devotees distrust organized religion as a whole.

### The House of Burgesses

An assembly of wealthy, land-owning lords and ladies. Cunning, assertive, and convinced of their own superiority, they can accomplish great things when acting as a single body. Unfortunately, their efforts are often undermined by individual squabbles, petty disputes, and their never-ending lust for power and prestige.

### What’s Really Going On?

A few short months after arriving at the mansion, Marius began an affair with a servant, Isobel. Young and inexperienced, she fell in love with Marius and threatened to reveal their relationship to his wife. Marius, fearing the loss of his status in the family, murdered Isobel and buried her body in a nearby grove (see [[The Mansion|11. Glade]]). What Marius did not realize was that young Isobel was a Half-Witch whose spirit cursed the Lockwood family and its descendants, infecting their water supply ([[The Mansion|9. Well]]) with [[Important Information|Moss Sickness]].

Soon after, Luciana began to show signs of the infection, her body slowly withering into an amalgam of human and plant. As her condition worsened, Marius withdrew from elite society, becoming more reclusive and paranoid by the day, even installing scrying mirrors to spy on his staff. Eventually he sealed the estate off from the outside world and ejected the rest of his servants. He then confined Luciana to their bedroom and forbade any visitors.

Eventually, Marius realized that in order to create a cure he would need a sample from a healthy relation — Anna. To ensure her safety, Luciana told her daughter to flee, and to drink a vial of Void Ichor ([[The Mansion|6. Workshop]]), which would erase all memory of her from the world. Aided by [[The Mansion|Mora the Cauchemora]], Anna fled into the Wood, drinking the potion only once she was a safe distance away from her father.

Not wishing to experience the memory-death of her beloved daughter, Luciana took her own life. Upon discovering his wife's corpse, Marius flew into a rage, and in a fit of madness took an experimental version of the cure for Moss Sickness, transforming him into a shambling mound. Today he roams the mansion, spreading the infection into nearly every room. Only [[The Mansion|16. Yellow Room]] remains untouched.

In time, the estate fell into disrepair, the neglected hedges returning to a wilder form. Decades later, Isobel's spirit continues to haunt the estate, wailing in sorrow and desperation.
