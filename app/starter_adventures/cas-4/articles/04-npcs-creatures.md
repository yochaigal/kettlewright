### Isobel

Isobel's spirit haunts the mansion grounds, furious and spiteful. She does not understand that she is dead, nor can she directly interact with the physical world. The arrival of her spirit is always precipitated by a drop in temperature, and exposed sources of light flicker. Anyone encountering Isobel makes an immediate WIL save to avoid feeling Panic until she is gone. She never remains in any location for more than a single Dungeon Exploration Cycle. Speaking her name while in her presence causes her to flee towards her body in [[The Mansion|11. Glade]]. If
Isobel's name is spoken aloud over her corpse, her spirit will be freed and the curse she has
wrought will finally end.

### Mossling

3 HP, 5 STR, 14 DEX, 12 WIL, toxic spores (d6, blast)

* Greenish bipedal growths spawned by various experiments. Intelligent.
* Susceptible to Moss Sickness and known to avoid the blue moss.
* **Critical Damage**: Painful chemical burns appear on target areas.

### Infected Mossling

1 HP, 2 STR, 16 DEX, 4 WIL, infected spores (d10, blast)

* Blue-green Mosslings infected with [[Important Information|Moss Sickness]].
* **Critical Damage**: Spores dig into exposed skin (2-in-6 chance the victim contracts Moss Sickness).

### Marius

Marius still roams the mansion as a Shambling Mound, spreading moss through every room except for [[The Mansion|16. Yellow Room]], which he avoids. He is always aggressive and cannot be reasoned with. If not already encountered, he is in [[The Mansion|6. Workshop]].

### Shambling Mound

**9 HP, 15 STR, 6 DEX, 12 WIL, tendrils (d8+d8)**

* If defeated, he will re-form in 1d4 hours. Only the Purple vial in the vat in [[The Mansion|6. Workshop]] can kill him permanently.
* Damage from non-bladed weapons is impaired.
* **Critical Damage**: Target is infected with [[Important Information|Moss Sickness]].
