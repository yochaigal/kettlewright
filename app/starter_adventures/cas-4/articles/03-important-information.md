The adventure should begin outside of the estate ([[The Mansion|1. Gates]]). Roll a Die of Fate to determine the weather and time that the party arrives. Make a Reaction Roll for creatures encountered throughout the adventure, except for when encountering [[NPCs & Creatures|Isobel]] and [[NPCs & Creatures|Marius]].

### Sensory Details

There is no artificial light anywhere in the mansion, and most wall sconces, candelabras, etc., are too dew-heavy to be lit. The interior smells heavily of damp earth and moss, like a forest floor after heavy rains.

### Mirrors

Mirrors are installed in every room except for the [[The Mansion|Workshop]]. The mirrors appear normal, but occasionally shimmer a phosphorescent green. In fact, each mirror acts as a viewpoint from the mirror in the [[The Mansion|18. Private Chambers]]. If the glued wood backing is removed from the back of a mirror, the reverse side of the glass glows in the dark, but does not provide true illumination.

### Blue Moss

Shed by Marius, especially if he is wounded. Harming the moss will draw him towards it (make a [[NPCs & Creatures|Dungeon Event]] roll). The moss covers nearly every surface of the mansion.

* Studying it reveals that it twitches every few minutes.
* It is drawn to nearby flesh, but will bend away from heat. A character touching the moss has a 4-in-6 chance of being infected with [[Important Information|Moss Sickness]].

### Moss Sickness

A viral disease that spreads through water droplets or blood contact. Symptoms start with infected individuals coughing up a bluish moss and eventually dying of asphyxiation over the course of about a month. The cure is detailed in Marius's journal ([[The Mansion|6. Workshop]]), but can only be implemented by a Greenwise or herbalist. A healthy relative of the patient is also required for the cure to work. If Isobel's spirit is put to rest, the curse will end and any living victims of the curse will be cured.
