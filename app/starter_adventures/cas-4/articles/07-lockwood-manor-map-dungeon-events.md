### Back Cover

_The Forgetting of Lockwood Manor_ is a gothic horror adventure about an estate where the crimes of its past residents live on, even after death!

Taking inspiration from the Winchester Mystery House, Swamp Thing, and Slavic folklore, this adventure is sure to provide a haunting experience for even the most seasoned characters, who must rely on their wits, gear, and resourcefulness to survive.

*Dare you enter the manor?*

The Forgetting of Lockwood Manor is the fourth entry in the Cairn Adventure Series by Yochai Gal, written for Cairn Second Edition.

ISBN: 978-1-971897-22-6
