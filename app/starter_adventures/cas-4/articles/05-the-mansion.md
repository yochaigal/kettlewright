### Entrance

#### 1. Gates

Ornate iron **gates**, heavy with rust, block the path. Thick, unmanicured **hedges** extend from the gates in all directions. A heavy **mist** enshrouds the area, obscuring the ground. _Do not roll for an encounter at this location._

* **Gates**: 12 feet high and ajar, but only wide enough for a slim person not wearing a backpack to slip through. The word “Lockwood” is engraved on top, and split in the middle.
	* The letter “O” in “Lock” is embedded with a small gem worth 25gp.
	* If the gate leaves are opened any wider, they emit a loud creaking sound that echoes throughout the area (make a [[NPCs & Creatures|Dungeon Event]] roll).
	* On the other side of the bars, the greenery is tall enough to obscure the view from the ground.
* **Hedges**: 10 feet in height, they extend in both directions for a few hundred feet before turning north in parallel.
	* If the party follows the hedges in either direction, they will eventually find themselves back at the gates. If the party tries to retrace their steps, the gates reappear almost immediately. This is an effect of Isobel's curse.
	* Climbing atop the hedge will reveal the mansion ([[The Mansion|3a. Entry]]) to the north, as well as a burnt building to the west ([[The Mansion|2. Servant’s Quarters]]).
* **Mist**: A trail leads north through the gates, then splits off to the west.
	* A large rusted lock lies near the gates, obscured by the mist. A red key is still inside the cylinder. If the key is removed, the key reverts to a puddle of useless fluid (see Key Syrup in [[The Mansion|6. Workshop]]).

![Map](https://cairnrpg.com/adventures/first-party/img/cas-4/gate.webp)

#### 2. Servant’s Quarters

A small collapsed **building** of tarnished wood and stone. An iron **stove** peeks out from amid the rubble. A small path **runs** north from the building.

* **Building**: Burnt beds and tables of shabby quality, originally supporting up to four servants.
* **Stove**: Blackened and bent as if from a small explosion.
	* Marius sent the servants away without the time to properly douse the stove. When the fire began, there was no one to douse it.
* **Rubble**: Broken masonry, bedframes, and charred personal items litter the floor.
	* A piece of wall-mirror survived the fire, though its frame did not. The back of the glass is unusually phosphorescent and glows in the dark.
	* Buried in the ashes is a red ruby necklace worth 150gp. A gift from Marius to Isobel, it bears her name in engraved text. Anyone wearing the necklace finds that they cannot remove it (even by force). It can only be removed by one who was wronged by the wearer, but later forgave them. If Isobel is put to rest, the necklace returns to normal.
* **Path**: From the back of the building is a path leading to the intersection of [[The Mansion|9. Well]] and [[The Mansion|10. Outdoor Theater]].

### First Floor

#### 3a. Entry

An asymmetrical, two-floor **mansion** with steep roof lines clad in fish-scale shingles. The facade looks built from an unusual greenish **wood**, and moss-covered stone steps lead onto an empty porch boasting two large red **doors**. The family **crest** is hung on the lintel above.

* **Mansion**: Heavy, thorny bushes hug the first floor of the building, obscuring a small window. Large bay windows are barely visible on the second floor.
	* Cutting the foliage can be accomplished within two turns, but only with heavy effort and the appropriate tools.
	* The small window leads into [[The Mansion|5. Kitchen]], but it is too dark to see anything inside. It is unlocked.
	* The bay windows lead to [[The Mansion|12. Dining Room]]. The blinds are closed.
	* Both [[The Mansion|10. Outdoor Theater]] and [[The Mansion|9. Well]] are visible on the west side.
* **Wood**: Plant growth penetrates the wood, tinging it green.
	* The surface of the mansion is extremely slippery and nearly impossible to climb without specialized tools and relevant skills.
* **Doors**: Closed but not locked. Leads to [[The Mansion|3b. Vestibule]].
* **Family crest**: A shield; the field is a grid of blue and gold squares. The gold squares contain red diamonds, and the blue squares contain white crosses with pointed bottoms.
	* The crest (bulky) is untouched by moss and can be removed with proper tools. It is worth 100gp to the right buyer.

#### 3b. Vestibule

A small but ornate waiting room with a pale **couch** pushed against the west wall. A single **mirror** is installed on the east wall. A short hallway leads to an open room to the north.

* **Couch**: Cracked leather. Pale blue moss grows through the cracks.
	* **Moss**: Created by Marius as he moved into [[The Mansion|4. Ballroom]].
* **Mirror**: A minor crack in the corner disturbs the glass. The gap it creates glows faintly, but only in total darkness.

![Map](https://cairnrpg.com/adventures/first-party/img/cas-4/entry.webp)

#### 4. Ballroom

A decadent ballroom and audience chamber with a tiled floor **mosaic**. Thick blue **vines** spill from a chandelier hanging in the center of the room. Blue **moss** grows from between the tiles near the west exit. A short set of stairs on the east side leads up to the second floor.

* **Mosaic**: Depicts a large crowd watching a dancer (Luciana) with her arm extended. On one finger is a real diamond ring. It can be pried loose in one turn and is worth 250gp.
* **Vines**: A Creeper in disguise. Attacks any living creature that crosses the room (DEX save to avoid).
* **Moss**: Shaped vaguely like footsteps. Created by Marius as he moved into [[The Mansion|5. Kitchen]].

![Map](https://cairnrpg.com/adventures/first-party/img/cas-4/ballroom.webp)

##### Creeper

**4 HP, 12 STR, 13 DEX, 5 WIL, tentacle vines (d6, blast)**

* Inert until a living creature comes near. Hates fire.
* **Drain**: Once bound, the victim loses 4 STR per round until they are completely drained of blood. The Creeper is defenseless when it is feeding.

#### 5. Kitchen

Rusted cutlery is scattered atop a moss-covered table. A small **mound** of plant life grows between a small table and metal sink. Blue moss grows out of the cracks in the floor. A square-shaped **mirror** is installed in the ceiling. A **window** on the south side of the room is obscured by overgrowth.

* **Mound**: Thick moss grows from the corpse of a human.
	* The body belongs to the house cook, Jacob. Suction-shaped marks are visible along his legs, as he was fatally wounded by the vines in [[The Mansion|4. Ballroom]] before crawling into the kitchen and expiring.
* **Moss**: Shaped vaguely like human footsteps.
	* Created by Marius as he moved from [[The Mansion|4. Ballroom]], through the [[The Mansion|kitchen]] towards [[The Mansion|6. Workshop]].
* **Mirror**: Operates like any other mirror in the mansion, providing a “top-down” view of the room when operated from [[The Mansion|18. Private Chambers]].
* **Window**: Obscured by foliage (see [[The Mansion|3a. Entry]]). Unlocked.

![Map](https://cairnrpg.com/adventures/first-party/img/cas-4/kitchen.webp)

#### 6. Workshop

A tall **cabinet** with 30 small drawers is installed on the west side. A long workbench stands in the center of the room, a large **vat** placed atop it. Moss grows in small cracks, starting at the south entrance and stops near the **workbench**. There are no mirrors in this room. If he has not yet been encountered, Marius is here.

* **Cabinet**: Each drawer holds a different sample of herb or other plant, and each takes one turn to examine (see table below). Only an expert in plant life could identify them all. If looking for a specific herb, roll the Die of Fate!
* **Workbench**: Topped by a vat and tube set, as well as a journal.
	* **Journal**: Filled with difficult-to-read script and high-level botany jargon. Details the research of an M. Lockwood, trying to find a cure for his wife's illness (the “Moss Sickness”). There is a definitive cure described in the text that any Greenwise can implement, provided a healthy relative of the infected is available. One sentence toward the end is quite clear: “Beginning trials on Anna tomorrow, as a precaution. Finally, the cure is at hand!” On the back of the journal is an appendix describing the workings of each potion held atop the Vat, but only their effects. No colors are indicated.
* **Vat**: Empty. Atop it is a rack bearing four bottles of liquid of varying colors.
* **Red**: Key Syrup. Pour into any lock; a single-use key will shape to match. 3 uses.
* **Black**: Void Ichor. Drink and all who knew you will forget you forever and ignore any references to your person, written or spoken. 1 use.
* **Blue**: Brittle Blade. Reacts with metal, sharpening its edge. The blade deals enhanced damage, then shatters on contact with blood. 2 uses.
* **Purple**: Flowering Death. Causes instant death to plant life, spreading and consuming all it touches until it makes contact with water. 1 use.

#### 7. Greenhouse

The glass panels of the ceiling are mostly intact, casting a kaleidoscope of light onto the overgrown **plants** growing here. A sheepskin shroud covers a small area, and a manure-like **fragrance** is faintly detectable nearby. A small **window** is open on the east side.

* **Plants**: Unusual varieties. Some respond to touch; others make giggling sounds.
* Shroud: Hidden beneath is Green Rot, a bioluminescent fungus. While alive, it can provide light equivalent to a candle until it is exposed to direct sunlight.
* **Fragrance**: Originates from a small yellow flower with purple leaves called the Dreaming Daisy. Spending more than one Dungeon Cycle here causes panic, as the victim suffers horrific hallucinations.
* **Window**: An angled view of [[The Mansion|16. Yellow Room]]. A rope made of sheets is visible.

![Map](https://cairnrpg.com/adventures/first-party/img/cas-4/greenhouse.webp)

#### 8. Western Hall

Narrow wooden walls and bare wooden floors. **Mirrors** are installed on opposite ends of the hall, which turns sharply halfway. Stairs ascend to the second level on the East side.

* **Mirrors**: The turning point halfway down the hall acts as a blind spot. The floor is unusually scuffed here.
	* Servants would meet here so as not to be overheard by Marius.


#### 9. Well

**Trees** line the north and east sides of a rectangular outdoor space. In the center is a small **well**. To the north is a small path through a thick copse of trees.

* **Trees**: Grow thick and close together. A larger person would struggle to pass through.
	* Stepping through the trees on the east side reveals a path to [[The Mansion|16. Yellow Room]] through the rope made of sheets hanging from an open window.
* **Well**: A functioning rope and bucket hang over the well.
	* A sip of water from the well causes intense visions followed by a brutal hangover for d4 hours. (Attacks are impaired and the drinker is deprived.) They also become infected with [[Important Information|Moss Sickness]].
	* The visions are at first an amorphous sense of fierce anger and loss, followed by the image of a young woman’s face, bloody sheets, and the color blue.

![Map](https://cairnrpg.com/adventures/first-party/img/cas-4/well.webp)

#### 10. Outdoor Theater

A wooden stage is constructed on the west side in front of a thick copse of mossy trees. A red curtain hangs over the stage, suspended from a rope strung between two long poles, and a large, angled **prism** is hung from a wooden pole near the east entry. Fine wooden **chairs** make a semi-circle around the stage.

* **Prism**: A powerful beam of light emanates from the object, pointed directly at the chairs and stage.
	* Shadows cast by the PCs from the prism dither and twitch eerily.
	* The prism is an artifact of great value (500gp). The device will not function during total darkness. Two individuals are required to safely move the prism.
* **Chairs**: In excellent condition despite their exposure to the elements.
	* Any character standing directly in the light of the prism finds they cannot move, as invisible bonds lace around their arms and legs. Moments later the curtain opens, and the shadows of the trapped onlookers bound onto the stage, where they morph into two “actors” ready to perform a play.
	* The shadow actors then pantomime a love affair between a man and a woman (Marius and Isobel) over three acts. It begins with their initial dalliance, is followed by Isobel’s pleas to elope, and ends with her eventual murder. The play is 3 dungeon cycles long, after which the audience is released. Disrupting the prism light or pointing a bullseye lantern at the shadow breaks their bond, dispersing the stage shadows as well.
	* If Isobel’s soul is laid to rest (see [[Warden Notes|Ending the Adventure]]), the theater returns to normal.

![Map](https://cairnrpg.com/adventures/first-party/img/cas-4/theater.webp)

#### 11. Glade

A small **clearing** amongst the trees. At the center is a large mound piled with **rocks**.

* **Clearing**: No moss grows here, not even on stones.
* **Rocks**: Heavy, they require 3 turns to clear. Beneath the rocks is blackened earth.
	* Beneath the rocks is an impromptu grave for Isobel. Her corpse is still fresh, as if she died from having her throat slit just a few hours ago. On her neck is a small tattoo of a [[Introduction|White Raven]].
	* The dirt is muddy and raw, as Isobel's necrotic energies have flowed into the water table below and into the well at [[The Mansion|9. Well]].

### Second Floor

#### 12. Dining Room

An enormous empty wooden table occupies the room. Six small **sculptures** are arranged in alcoves along the east wall. Single mirrors are installed on the east and west walls. An empty chandelier hangs **from** the ceiling. Large bay **windows** face the south. A set of stairs descend on the west side.

* **Sculptures**: Busts of men and women of the Lockwood line. All but two are broken. One of the surviving busts is of a serious-looking woman with shoulder-length hair. The other is that of a high-cheeked young girl with long hair. Both are bulky and valuable only if sold unbroken (100gp each).
* **Chandelier**: Free from vines and can be re-lit.
* **Windows**: Completely covered by vines and foliage.

![Map](https://cairnrpg.com/adventures/first-party/img/cas-4/dining-room.webp)

#### 13. Hall of Rooms

Branched passages of gaudy yellow, wood wet with rot. Bluish **moss** covers nearly every spot of floor. On the east side are two rooms, one with a **red door** with an upside-down broom leaning against the outside and another with a **blue door**. On the west side is a **yellow** door.

* **Moss**: Only the floor nearest to [[The Mansion|16. Yellow Room]] is free from moss.
* **Red Door**: Locked but easily forced. The broom can be moved aside with ease (see [[The Mansion|Cauchemora]]). See [[The Mansion|14. Red Room]].
* **Blue Door**: Unlocked. See [[The Mansion|15. Blue Room]].
* **Yellow Door**: Locked but easily forced. See [[The Mansion|16. Yellow Room]].

![Map](https://cairnrpg.com/adventures/first-party/img/cas-4/hall-of-rooms.webp)

#### 14. Red Room

The **air** here is fresh despite the lack of ventilation. A wooden **privy** is installed on the east side, opposite a small bureau with **figurines** arranged on top.

* **Air**: The walls and furniture are free of mold or age.
* **Privy**: Perfectly clean. Something shiny glints inside, six feet down.
	* After murdering Isobel, Marius tossed the blood-stained knife here.
* **Figurines**: Typical except for one, an old woman (Mora, see opposite) with goat-like eyes. She will remain in disguise until she can determine the party's intentions, but will transform if picked up.

![Map](https://cairnrpg.com/adventures/first-party/img/cas-4/red-room.webp)

##### Cauchemora

An immortal spirit bound to serve a family so long as their descendants live. Their origin is the Wood, and they long to return to it. They take many forms, but are most often found as minuscule humans or black cats (their goat-like eyes never change). They love beer, hay, and whistling. They find sunshine uncomfortable and prefer the company of birds and squirrels. Household items (such as brooms) placed against doors act as a ward against the Cauchemara, and are often used to control their movement.

When angered, they can cause terrible nightmares and delusions in the minds of their enemies. Like people, they can be good or evil. They cannot be harmed by normal means, and have perfect memory.

##### Mora the Cauchemora

Mora is not her true name, but simply a nickname that Anna provided her. She quite likes it. She will return kindness with kindness.

When Luciana became ill, Mora cast a protective ritual on Anna, staving off the Moss Sickness. As Marius became increasingly paranoid, he began to suspect that Mora was working against him. Indeed, she provided Anna with the Key Syrup and Void Ichor ([[The Mansion|6. Workshop]]) needed to escape her father. As punishment, Marius trapped Mora in [[The Mansion|14. Red Room]] by placing an upside-down broom against the door of the room. If the broom is ever moved, the ritual keeping Mora trapped there will be broken, and she will be free to move about the estate.

Mora happily reveals everything she knows about the history of the Lockwood family for a bit of food (especially hay). As a spirit, she is not affected by the effects of the Void Ichor and knows everything that has transpired between Marius and Isobel, and Anna and Luciana. Although she knows how to end Isobel's curse and will happily inform characters that are kind to her, she will not act against Marius. Once freed she will wish for nothing more than to leave the cursed mansion and find Annafranax as quickly as possible.

#### 15. Blue Room

The door to this room is painted blue. A simple bed is arrayed in the center of the room perpendicular to a small set of drawers. A tall mirror is installed on the north side.

* **Bed**: Sheets appear unused. The mattress below is stained blood-red.
	* Isobel was stabbed to death on this bed by Marius, who cleaned the sheets (but not the bed) before stashing the murder weapon in the privy ([[The Mansion|14. Red Room]]).
* **Drawers**: Bed linens and pillow cases are folded neatly within.
* **Mirror**: Unlike every other mirror in the mansion, this one is completely mundane.
	* Attached to the wall; with heavy effort (enough to trigger a Dungeon Event), the frame will swing open, revealing a secret tunnel to [[The Mansion|18. Private Chambers]].

#### 16. Yellow Room

The door to this room is painted yellow. A bare **bed** is placed near an open
**window** on the west side, and a small dollhouse sits atop a small table
nearby. Weather-beaten children’s toys are scattered across the wooden
**floor**.

* **Bed**: The bed sheets are missing.
	* A small journal is stuffed between the mattress and frame. Written by a 13-year-old girl, it details the family’s move to the mansion and the early stages of her mother’s illness. It ends with remarks about her father exhibiting strange behavior. The final entry reads: “_I am scared. Mother is helping me get away very soon" She says to run as far as I am able, and once I am free, to drink the black syrup from father's workshop…_" The entry is signed: _Annafranax Milo Lockwood_.
* **Window**: A curved awning protects the room from elements.
	* Looks out onto [[The Mansion|7. Greenhouse]] and the copse of trees adjacent to [[The Mansion|9. Well]].
	* Rotting bed sheets hang from the windowsill, touching the earth below. The sheets will break if an adult tries to climb them.
* **Dollhouse**: A perfect replica of the mansion. The secret passage connecting [[The Mansion|15. Blue Room]] and [[The Mansion|18. Private Chambers]] is clearly visible in the toy structure.
* **Floor**: Untouched by corruption. Marius will not enter this room under any circumstances and will ignore any who enter it.

![Map](https://cairnrpg.com/adventures/first-party/img/cas-4/yellow-room.webp)

#### 17. Library

Rotted **bookshelves** form winding paths throughout the room. A writing **desk** sits along the west wall, bedecked with a set of quills and ink **bottles**.

* **Bookshelves**: Mostly rotted. What’s left can be discovered with a thorough search (one book discovered per turn).
	* *Field Guide to Natural Wonders*: Any blood spilled on the page displays an illustration of the wounded creature, as well as basic facts and weaknesses.
	* *Decorum of Stately Pleasures*: Normal-looking. Shake it to reveal its real contents: smut.
	* *Survive*: Small and blank, but smells faintly of cinnamon. Pages can be eaten as Rations (5 uses). Can become a thick stew with a little water. Never goes bad.
* **Desk**: Sturdy but beginning to rot. The central drawer is unlocked.
	* **Drawer**: Contains various records for home goods, deliveries, and food. A list of household staff is written at the bottom: Jacob, Lester, and one that is crossed out. Only the letters "...bel" are still visible.
* **Bottles**: Labeled with symbols.
	* **Circle**: Blood-red. Opens a harmless fist-sized hole in any object or creature until it is washed off. 3 uses.
	* **Butterfly**: Bright purple. An animal drawn with this ink comes to life as a familiar, bound to whomever drew it. Attempts to draw anything else will not work (the ink returns to the bottle). 1 use.
	* **Blank**: Completely transparent; the bottle appears empty. Whatever it touches becomes invisible. 4 uses.
	* **Dash**: Darkest black. Quickly nullifies any of the other inks (including itself). 6 uses.

#### 18. Private Chambers

A bed occupies the center of the room, a humanoid **shape** pressed into its center. A small writing **desk** and chair are pushed against the east wall, below a curtained **window**. A long mirror **hangs** on the south wall, and a hanging **shelf** is laden with personal effects.

* **Shape**: The desiccated corpse of a woman lies atop the mattress, a shard of glass in one hand.
	* The woman is Luciana. She cut her wrists with broken glass so as to avoid the pain of forgetting her own daughter due to the effects of the Void Ichor ([[The Mansion|6. Workshop]]). Her corpse is an amalgam of decayed human flesh and plant residue.
* **Desk**: Contains moldering clothes and useless personal effects. There are only a few items of value.
	* A token bearing the Milo family crest (a green ouroboros set against a red field). Luciana wore it on the day she married Marius.
	* An ornate necklace valued at 532gp.
* **Window**: Opening the blinds displays a view of the east hedges, surrounded by forest. It is a two-story drop to the ground, cushioned only by thorny overgrowth.
* **Mirror**: The mirror shows no reflection. A small section of the mirror is missing.
	* If the glass held by Luciana is returned to the mirror, it heals and displays a live view for any room in the mansion, even when that room is shrouded in total darkness.
* **Shelf**: Mostly small items of no value, as well as a standing illustration of Marius.
	* Beneath the shelf is a switch that activates the secret passage to [[The Mansion|15. Blue Room]], which swings open.
	* Anna would use this passage to visit with her mother after Marius locked Luciana away.

### Dungeon Exploration Cycle

* Each turn, a PC may move ~40ft and take one action. Using the action to move instead allows up to 120ft, but risks a [[NPCs & Creatures|Dungeon Events roll]] (see below).
* A Dungeon Events roll is triggered when the party spends more than one turn in a room, moves haphazardly, enters a new area or level, or creates a disturbance.
* Actions are any non-passive activities: searching, forcing doors, listening, disarming traps, combat, spellcasting, resting, etc. Loud or noticeable actions may trigger an encounter. Some actions take multiple turns to complete.
* Searching one object or location exhaustively takes one turn and reveals hidden treasure, traps, or secret doors. Larger or more complex areas may take longer. Searching first is safer, but costs time.
* Resting one turn restores all HP, but requires light and a safe location. Resting does not restore Fatigue.
