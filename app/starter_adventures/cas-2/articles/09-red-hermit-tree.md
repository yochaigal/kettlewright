title: "Red Hermit Tree"
type: Note
parent: "Warden Information"
---
Grows upside down, hanging from the ceiling in half-submerged caves. Needs water and methane to survive. Its gourd-like fruit is initially sweet to eat, but after a day turns brown and smells of carrion. The fruit is a delicacy, and if eaten fresh can close wounds and revitalize the blood (recovers 1 STR and removes deprivation, once per day). The tree that grows in the **[[Blue Mouth Caves]]** is dying due to lack of water, a result of the temporary dam built by the expeditionary team in the [[C9. Blood Olm Lair]].
