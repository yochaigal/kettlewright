An enormous **tree** hangs from the ceiling, leaving its roots exposed. A small volume of **water** is pooled below. Near the South **wall**, two **corpses** lie face-down.

- **Tree**: A Red Hermit Tree. It is dying from lack of water.
	- If water is restored to this room and the roots are fully immersed, the tree will survive and bear fruit again and the Olms from [[C8. Olm Den]] will survive.
	- The uppermost branches of the tree are spackled in guano. The tree is typically covered in bats, but they have all fled to [[C6. Bat Room]] when the tree stopped bearing fruit.
- **Water**: A small pool is carved into the ground at a slope. There is enough water to replenish the party’s supplies, but no more. Rotten fruit floats on top.
	- The water can be restored by destroying the dam in [[C9. Blood Olm Lair]]. 
- **Corpses**: The remains of **Faraj** and **Oren**. Dismembered by a Blood Olm.
	- Appear dead for only at least three days.
	- The larger of the two (**Oren**) carries a small knife (d6), a torch (1 use left), and 47gp. The smaller (**Faraj**) carries a compass (55gp) and a blunt shovel (d6).
