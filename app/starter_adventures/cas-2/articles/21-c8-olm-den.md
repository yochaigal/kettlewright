Warm, cramped. 10-14 **Olms** sleeping, eating, whispering. The **walls** are packed mud painted with bursts of color. A small **divider** on the Northeast side is filled with water. The room is very humid and warm. Rotting fruit and excrement litters the floor.

- **Olms**: Sickly. A wide variety of sizes. Some appear unmoving.
	- Ignore the PCs, unless they have fresh fruit on them (they can smell it).
	- Cannot speak, but show signs of high intelligence.
- **Walls**:  Swirls of color made from packed guano and fruit. They tell the following story:
	- The Olms (painted here with eyes) came down the river from the mountains, making a home in the caves.
	- The Olms found the fruit tree, and there was much rejoicing. 
	- An Olm eats the flesh of a tube worm, then is chased away by its former clan.
- **Divider**: Clear water bubbles up from a spring, surrounded by a waist-high mud wall.
	- The spring is still very healthy: fish swim, vegetation grows, and methane vents release periodic warm gas in the bottom. The water is safe to drink.
	- If the mud wall is destroyed, the spring will flood [[C8. Olm Den]], [[C3. Tube Worm Hive]], and [[C2. Cave Entrance]], partially restoring life to the caverns. However without restoring water to the Red Hermit Tree in [[C7. Atrium]] these Olms will still perish.
