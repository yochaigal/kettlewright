The corridor to this room is blocked by a wall of **mud** from floor to ceiling. White **shoe prints** lead into the corridor. 

- **Mud**: Perfectly smooth packed and dried mud. Can be cut through by hand in an hour or in just a few minutes with digging tools. 
- **Shoe Prints**: White from guano. Lead directly into the wall of mud, as if it weren’t there. 
	- Speaking loudly here will attract the occupant of [[C10b. Refuge]], who will call out. Due to the mud however her voice will be heavily muffled.
