A primary water source for the Cities, flowing through a tight canyon called the **Fingers**. In recent months the ancient river has lost much of its water, revealing a [[C2. Cave Entrance|large, blue-ringed cave]]. As a result of this drawdown the water levels in the cave have also dropped significantly.
