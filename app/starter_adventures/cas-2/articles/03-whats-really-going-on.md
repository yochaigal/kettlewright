A few days after arriving, the original expedition team discovered the **Red Hermit Tree** and the miraculous healing properties of its fruit. To more easily harvest the tree they dammed the river in **[[C9. Blood Olm Lair|C9]]**. Once the fruit was harvested the bats fled their tree home to **[[C6. Bat Room|C6]]**, and the Olms became very hungry. Some took to eating **Tube Worm** flesh, triggering a latent carnivorous impulses deep within. Meanwhile, **High Lector Geteli** has gone mad after eating Tube Worm flesh, and the local cave explorer **Jhel-Fries** is holed up in **[[C10b. Refuge|C10b]]**.
