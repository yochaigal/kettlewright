A **rope ladder** dangles from a hole in the ceiling. In the Southwest corner is an **iron box**. Dead fish stick out from the muddy **ground**, which slopes to higher ground from the East exit.

- **Rope**: Tunnel to the outside ([[C1. Campsite]]). The ladder and tunnel are both 12ft long. Like the rest of the room, the tunnel is very muddy and appears newly made.
- **Iron box**: Two pitons and dried rations (2 uses left).
- **Ground**: White, guano-flecked shoe prints traverse the room.
