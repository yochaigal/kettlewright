Grey, barren corridors connecting **[[T2. Columbarium|T2]]** to **[[T6. Waste Disposal|T6]]** and **[[T7. Light Room|T7]]**. U-shaped bars extend from the terminus of each hallway. Twisted, they can open a hatch to each. In the central corridor, a long **cloth** is covered by some rubble.

- **Cloth**: Among the debris is a very well-preserved skeleton of a tall, bird-like humanoid, wearing a small red crest on its head. Its throat is ripped open, and it is wrapped in **[[Relics & Items|Moon Cloth]]**. It carries a small, **[[Relics & Items|Curved Dagger (d6)]]** with a red jewel on it.
