In the center of this square-shaped room is a square **well**, waist-high. Three **nozzles** protrude from the south wall, with no visible controls anywhere in sight. A **drain** and grate are installed beneath each. On the west wall, two large horizontal **seams** are arranged in parallel a few feet apart.

- **Well**: Hundreds of feet deep. A light source reveals ancient refuse floating in the water at the bottom.
- **Nozzle**: Automatic showers that spray scalding hot water whenever weight is placed on its drain. A single chirp from a corner light sconce is the only warning.
- **Drains**: Pulling up the grate in the **Southwest** corner triggers a hatch in the **west** wall, opening a **secret door** to **[[T4. Maintenance Tunnel|T4]]**.
- **Seams**: A hatch to **[[T4. Maintenance Tunnel|T4]]**. Like its companions in **[[T2. Columbarium|T2]]** and **[[T7. Light Room|T7]]**, the seams are too narrow for a crowbar or blade, and  there are no obvious handles, buttons, or levers in sight.
