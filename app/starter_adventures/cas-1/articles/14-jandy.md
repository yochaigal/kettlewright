A middle-aged, frumpy Scrivener and tithingman for the Cities. Regularly visited Isthmus Town to collect the tithe from Reeve Trellis. He travels freely by horse and wagon, as no bandit would dream of attacking someone bearing the livery of the Cities.  

- Keeps a regular room at the Stuffed Inn but was scheduled to leave on the same day that he visited Deadmill. His horse and wagon are still at the village stables, however.
- Friendly with Giva, whom he interviewed for a work on the region’s past.
- If rescued, he will say that his last memories were of first entering the building from the West entrance. Noting the fine paddles of the mill through the East window, he exited via the South door to investigate. Failing to notice the quicksand he fell in, died, and then was reborn inside the mill.
- Keeps a record of tithes in his side bag.
