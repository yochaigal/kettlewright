The East wall overflows with intricate **carvings**. 

- **Carvings** (in order from topmost to bottom):
	- Humanoid figures stand on a tower, pointing towards an object falling from the sky. It looks like an enormous beetle.
	- Tall, winged figures wearing enormous headdresses stream out of a hole at the front of the “beetle.” They are followed by a pair of crouched, lizard-like creatures. 
	- The lizard-like creatures rip apart the humanoid onlookers, and overwhelm the tower.
	- The winged figures stand above a crowd of kneeling humanoids, their lizard “pets” standing aside.
