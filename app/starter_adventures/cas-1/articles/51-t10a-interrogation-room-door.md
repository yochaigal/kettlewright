A short hallway connects **[[T9. Chamber of Worship|T9]]** to a large metallic door. A single hooked gash is cut into the right-hand side, and a small **window** is built into the door six feet from the ground. Adjacent to the door is a metallic **handle** (left, facing the door). 
- **Door**: Normally opened only by slotting the **Curved Dagger** from **[[T4. Maintenance Tunnel|T4]]** into the gash.
- **Window**: Reveals the room’s contents in their entirety. Thick but breakable glass.
- **Handle**: Opens a drawer, similar to a mail slot. It is slick with blood.
