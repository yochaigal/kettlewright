A short dirt path connecting **Isthmus Town** to the **Stone Forest**, where it quickly disappears. **Deadmill** is visible through the trees, just at the edge of the wood. If **Annafranax** [[The Return of Vuk|has returned]], the grove and the mill will be gone, replaced by a massive sinkhole. See “**[[If the Gate has Opened...]]**”.
