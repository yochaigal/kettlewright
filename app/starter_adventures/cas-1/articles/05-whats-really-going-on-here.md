What appears at first to be a set of interconnected events are instead *simple coincidences*. Aldra’s fate and that of the man at Deadmill are in fact completely unrelated. While both *The Return of Vuk* and *The Swallowed Man* share some of the characters and locations, the preceding events have nothing to do with each other.
