A single-room shack on the north side of town. Built from wood more weathered and older than the wood used in other buildings in town. The **door** is painted bright purple. Dried vegetation is stuffed into the top of the door.

- **Door**: Recently painted. Giva believes it protects against evil spirits. 
- **Vegetation**: Dried wolfsbane and other herbs to ward off werewolves.

### Interior

A heated wood stove dominates the west wall, and a small bed is tucked beneath a window on the north side. In the kitchen, a table is set with two glasses and a **wine** bottle.

- **Wine**: A very old vintage (worth 50gp). 
	- Giva will say that she sets out the wine “for company.” She also makes it very clear that the party is not who she is waiting for. 
	- She will admit that the person she is waiting on (Jandy) missed their last rendezvous; he was meant to stop by on his way out of town nearly a week ago.
