A religious sect that worships Rivulus, god of Rivers and Lakes. Even the unaffiliated are familiar with the group’s initiation ceremony, a ritual drowning that occasionally kills participants. Survivors are dubbed “Tideborn” and adopt a new, water-related moniker (Carp, Reed, Oxbow, and so on). Only clergy need participate in the ritual, and in recent years their following among the rural folk has increased, especially among villages near bodies of water.
