Freshly cut trees, smeared with orange and black **gunk**. The sound of great movement and **treefall** can be heard as something makes its way towards the [[1. Bartermoot]] festival, the sound of a **bell** ringing in its wake. 

- **Gunk:** Contagion. Direct contact with skin causes infection. Spending more than 1 hour in this area also promotes infection (save STR or become infected).
- **Treefall:** The Chitin Knight, making its way towards [[1. Bartermoot]], spreading contagion as it cuts down trees in its path. It moves slowly, with a singular purpose: to spread Contagion. 
- **Bell:** Formerly attached to the tower door at the [[5. Abandoned Tower]], it has become fused onto one of the Chitin Knight’s appendages.

### Chitin Knight
12 HP, 2 Armor, 14 STR, 8 DEX, 6 WIL, arm spike (d10, _blast_)

- A horrific insectoid hybrid of insect and tree, spreading contagion in its wake. One of its legs has merged with blood-red wood, the rest of its body is black as midnight.
- **Critical Damage:** Target is impaled, and infected with contagion.
