A modest gray building sits atop a small hill, the roof completely collapsed into the structure. The front **door** is shut and flanked by windows on either side. The **interior** of the building is visible, provided there is sufficient light. A small copse of orange-tinged **trees** lies felled to the southwest of the ruin.

- **Door:** Dented and fused shut. In front of the door is a line of broken, ashy powder. A burnt footprint is scorched into the stone floor. 
	- The powder is a ward against trespassers, used to destroy alchemical experiments and keep them from prying rivals. The scorched footprint is from a Bloodhound that stepped on the powder, initiating the explosion.
- **Windows:** One of the windows is smashed, glass scattered across the lintel and surrounding area (save DEX or take 1 STR damage, if climbing through). The other window opens just fine.
- **Interior:** There is a massive hole where a large section of roof used to be. Metal and wood are scattered across the floor and amid broken furniture. A large pile of rubble obscures the back half of the room.
	- The body of Taros Quill is here, buried beneath the rubble. It takes 3 Turns to clear enough rubble to access the body. On his right forearm is a tattoo of a red flower, representing the Oath of the Marchguard.
	- Careful examination of his partially-charred remains should result in sufficient evidence to prove the man died from the building’s collapse, the explosion, or both.
- **Trees:** Orange-tinged footprints lead to the West ([[9. Last Stand]]). There is no trail here.
