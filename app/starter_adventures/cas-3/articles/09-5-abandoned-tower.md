A clearing, bursting with overgrown **foliage**. A **tower** infested with **ivy** is visible on the other side of a wall of thick undergrowth. Surrounding its foundation are dozens of **statues** made from plant life. A mist-covered **trail** on the northwest side of the tower leads deeper into the forest. A thick, bramble-covered path leads south. A well-trod trail heads east, uphill, towards the sound of water.

- **Foliage:** The plant life is impossibly thick here. Extensive chopping and cutting is required to reach the tower. Green-tinged statues are visible through gaps in the vegetation. They look like people in various states of activity. 
- **Tower:** Two stories high and covered in leafy ivy. A wooden door is present at its base, the entryway shrouded in dark. 
	- A tiled rooftop is visible, along with a small window. Closer inspection via spyglass or similar instrument would reveal a faint light through the window. Easily scalable due to the presence of heavy ivy. A fall would bruise, but no more. On the second floor is a shutterless window.
- **Door:** Stands slightly ajar, as if closed but not latched before being abandoned. Enters onto the first floor. 
- **Ivy:** Creeping vines. Appear inert, but slowly encroach upon distracted prey. If a character climbs the vines, they will wait until the perfect moment to strike. 
- **Statues:** Made of thick forest detritus, leaves, soil, sticks, and moss. They are shaped like people in varying expressions of terror: running away from the tower, screaming in agony, with their arms in the air as if to protect themselves.
- **Trail:** The mist is so thick that torches or lanterns are needed to pass. A sound not unlike distant bells chimes towards the northwest.  

### First Floor
The **contents** in this room have been completely destroyed and upended by an explosion of plant life. Parts of bookcases, chairs, cooking pots, etc. are distributed roughly throughout, and a latched **trapdoor** is cut into the ceiling on the north side, 12ft above the first floor.

- **Contents:** What was once a serene kitchen or workshop was undone by a force so violent it tore a hole through a wrought iron chamber pot, a tree branch erupting from its bottom. Amid the destruction is a broken rope ladder and a fine pair of Work Gloves. 
- **Trapdoor:** Latched from the other side of the ceiling. Parts of a rope ladder still hang in ragged chunks from the wood. 

### Second Floor
A small table cluttered with **instruments** is arranged on the west side of the room, opposite an open **window** that overlooks the forest. A latched **trapdoor** is built into the floor on the north side of the room. Wooden **boxes** are stacked neatly on the south side. The space is clean and untouched by the destruction of the floor below.

- **Instruments:** Ceramic bowls, alembics, and other varied kinds of glasswork. A knowledgeable character could identify what was being prepared (for example, a potion or alloy) but not the exact solution or product. There is residue of an orange powder.
- **Window:** A hidden path is visible just beyond the overgrowth east of the tower, leading to a slanted gazebo astride the river ([[6. Slanted Gazebo]]). 
- **Trapdoor:** A 12ft drop onto the first floor. Parts of a rope ladder still hang in ragged chunks.
- **Boxes:** Three small boxes. They contain: A pair of unused [[1. Bartermoot|Bubblemasks]], an empty, orange-tinged vial, and a can of Fire Oil.
