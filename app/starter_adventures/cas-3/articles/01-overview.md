**Garden Day**
_14th of Sunrise, 7728._
_Two weeks into the dry season, during Bartermoot. Warm & Breezy._
- Ask Each PC: **Why are you attending the Bartermoot? What are you hoping to find here?**

This adventure is structured as a forestcrawl. Refer to the relevant exploration procedures found in the [Warden’s Guide](/second-edition/wardens-guide/wilderness-exploration/) (pg. 56) and the [Player’s Guide](/second-edition/players-guide/procedures/#wilderness-exploration) (pg. 70).

**The Holiday of Bartermoot**

A day-long festival where representatives from the smaller towns and villages trade steel for lumber in ritual arrangement with the denizens of the Wood. The Marchguard keeps the peace. There are many such festivals located throughout the land.

**What’s Really Going On?**

Taros Quill, formerly of the Marchguard, broke his Oath and fled east. An Aurifex, he had altered his mind to better serve the Guard, but those machinations had eventually driven him mad. He has built a cult following around him somewhere in the Tegny Wood, where he plans on “freeing” humanity from the scourge of civilization. In order to enact these plans, he needed rare reagents, which he stole from the Guard.

But then, disaster struck! In his attempts to create a new kind of human, Quill created disease that destroys everything it touches. After his followers were transformed into plant abominations, he fled to the [[8. Workshop|Workshop]]. A Bloodhound followed him there, tripping an alchemical ward set by Quill, killing him. The Hound survived, but was later transformed into the [[9. Last Stand|Chitin Knight]].

**Contagion**

Creatures directly exposed to the Chitin Knight or an infected entity (typically via physical contact) must make a STR save or die horribly in 1d6 Turns, transforming into an abominable hybrid of plant and animal. (affected players should roll up a new character). They can be saved only if the infected body part is removed (within 1d4 Turns), or cured through magical means.
