### The Stolen Bell [bulky, charges 3/3]
A bronze bell green with age. When rung, every drowned thing within a mile turns toward it.
- **Recharge**: Hang it a night in the abbey's belfry.

### Pilgrim's Token [petty]
A tin badge of the old road. Ferrymen carry its bearer for free.
