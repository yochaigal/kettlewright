# Illustrated Bestiary tokens

One token for each monster of the Cairn bestiary, named after the monster (`black-bear.webp` is the Black Bear).

- Art: [Oozejar](https://oozejar.com), from the [Illustrated Bestiary](https://gourdin-konbo-club.itch.io/illustrated-bestiary)
  published by Gourdin Konbo Club, licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
- The tokens come from [Cairn for Atlas VTT](https://github.com/ByteMirror/atlas-vtt-cairn) (CC BY-SA 4.0), where the
  drawings were cropped to round tokens and set on a tinted background.
- Changes for Kettlewright: renamed after the bestiary entries and scaled down to at most 400 × 400 pixels.

These files are shared under the same licence, [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
