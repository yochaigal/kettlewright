Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy explains what Kettlewright (“we”) collects when you use kettlewright.com and the Kettlewright Discord app, why, who can see it, and how to remove it. Kettlewright is free and shows no ads. We do not sell your data and we use no analytics or tracking. This policy is part of our Terms of Service.

What we collect

  • Your account: username, email address and password. The password is stored only as a secure hash. We also keep when the account was created, when you last signed in, and whether your email is confirmed. If you turn on two-step sign-in, we keep the one-time codes we email you, hashed, for up to 10 minutes.
  • What you create: characters and their inventories and notes, companions, parties, campaigns, articles and maps, whiteboard drawings and fog, and dice roll history.
  • Images you upload: character and companion portraits, images in articles, and images on the whiteboard.
  • Discord, if you connect it: your Discord user ID and display name, linked to your account. Discord’s sign-in is used only to confirm who you are (the “identify” permission); we do not store Discord’s access tokens. When a Warden binds a channel to a party, we store that server and channel ID and the character you select there. To avoid running a command twice, we keep a record of each Discord command for 24 hours.
  • Whether you are online: while Kettlewright is open, your browser tells the server every 30 seconds, so your Warden can see who is connected. This lasts about a minute and is not stored in the database.
  • Technical logs: like most websites, the server and its hosting provider may record technical information such as IP addresses and requests, to run and protect the Service.

Cookies and storage in your browser

  • A session cookie keeps you signed in and protects your forms. If you choose “remember me”, a cookie keeps you signed in across visits.
  • A language cookie remembers your language for 180 days.
  • Your browser’s local storage remembers preferences on that device, such as dark mode, 3D dice and whiteboard settings. It stays on your device.

We use no advertising or analytics cookies.

Who can see your information

  • Character sheets can be read by anyone who has their link.
  • Party pages can be read by signed-in users who have their link. Party members, and the party’s Warden, see what the party shares, such as rolls, the whiteboard and revealed articles. Private rolls stay private.
  • Your email address is not shown to other users.
  • In Discord, what the app posts in a channel (for example public rolls, character cards and party summaries) can be read by everyone who can read that channel. Replies marked as only visible to you are shown to you alone.
  • Kettlewright’s public API returns game data such as backgrounds and tables, never user data.

Services we rely on

To run, Kettlewright uses a few other services, which receive only what they need:

  • a hosting provider, which stores the database and uploaded images;
  • an email provider, to send confirmation, password reset and sign-in emails;
  • Google reCAPTCHA, when it is turned on, on the sign-up page only, to stop automated sign-ups. See Google’s Privacy Policy;
  • font and code delivery networks (Google Fonts, cdnjs, unpkg, jsDelivr and esm.sh), from which your browser loads fonts and libraries. Like any website, they receive your IP address and browser details;
  • Discord, if you use the Discord app. See Discord’s Privacy Policy.

We may disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your information while you have an account. You can change it at any time, export your characters and campaigns, and delete your account on your Account page. Deleting your account removes your account, characters, parties, campaigns, Discord connection and uploaded images; images that other remaining content still uses are kept for that content. Disconnecting Discord, on the Discord page, removes your Discord link, channel selections and the bindings you created. If the server keeps backups, deleted information may remain in them until they are replaced.

Children

Kettlewright is not meant for children under 13, and they may not create an account. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, let us know and we will delete it.

Security

Passwords are hashed, connections are encrypted, and two-step sign-in is available. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you do not use elsewhere.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when what we collect or how we use it changes, and change the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will announce it on the website or on GitHub.

Contact

Questions or requests about your information can be raised by opening an issue on GitHub. Issues are public, so do not include personal information there: we will reply on the issue and arrange a private way to continue. You can delete your account and disconnect Discord yourself at any time.
